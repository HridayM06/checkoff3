# Reflection: Learning About Benchmarking With AI

## How I approached the topic
- Starting question: c vs python speed, how is it different?
- How it developed: Went from "which is faster?" to "why?" to "what else matters?" and to some benchmark algorithms and how they look structurally.

## Representative prompts
1. benchmark design for searching arrays
2. Give me a step by step walkthrough
3. im confused on the time complexity, could you explain in this context and generally?

## How I engaged with AI
- Asked for a skeleton of code and how to understand it in a slow walkthrough.
- Asked for time complexities adjustment and how it changes based on context on the problem at hand
- Reasoned with real world examples to understand potential applications

## What I'd do differently
- Ask for caveats earlier, verify the vectorization claim with compiler flags, and/or also understand time complexities a bit better before understanding this topic.
